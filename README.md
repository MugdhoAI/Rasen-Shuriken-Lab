# Rasen Shuriken Lab

Testing ground for new modes for [Wind Style Rasen Shuriken](https://github.com/MugdhoAI/Wind-Style-Rasen-Shuriken). Nothing here is final. A mode graduates to the main repo only after it has been tried and approved.

## What is being tested

**Mode 6: Wave.** Two hands: Orbs + Link.

- Your hand tracking is drawn on screen: dots on every joint, lines along the fingers.
- A glowing orb floats above each palm.
- Hold both hands up and an energy wave flows between the two orbs.

Modes 1 to 5 are the current main repo build, unchanged: Classic, Wind Shuriken, Magnet Shuriken, Lava Shuriken and Dual.

## Try it

Open `index.html` through the live page for this repo, or serve the folder locally and open it in Chrome. It needs a webcam and camera permission. Press 1 to 6 or tap the buttons at the bottom to switch modes. Make a fist to charge, open your hand to release.
